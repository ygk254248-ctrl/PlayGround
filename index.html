↻ Best played in landscape on phones

How to Play

Move & Climb

  • A/D or ←→ — run
  • Space / W / ↑ — jump
  • Jump again mid-air — double jump
  • ↓ — dodge roll (invincible while rolling)
  • Shift / C — dash
  • Hold toward a wall in mid-air, then jump — wall jump

Strike

  • J / Z — light attack (chain a 3-hit combo)
  • K / X — heavy attack (huge knockback)
  • Attacks work in the air too
  • Esc / P — pause · M — mute

The Tower

  • Follow the torchlit ledges upward — the boss waits at the summit
  • Brave the slimes, goblins and storm-bats; spikes and fire jets bite
  • Look for gilded archways — hidden treasure hides behind them
  • Braziers save your progress. Fall, and you rise again there
